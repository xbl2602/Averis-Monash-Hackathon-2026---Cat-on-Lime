* All participants will receive a LinkedIn badge and e-certificate of participation, provided the participants have participated under a registered team and submitted their project.

  * Finalists will receive a separate finalist e-certificate in recognition of reaching the Final Round.

  * Cash prizes will be awarded to the Top 3 finalist teams after all rounds have been completed.
